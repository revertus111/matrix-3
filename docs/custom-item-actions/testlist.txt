8. Click `Wear`. Confirm it still equips the physical backpack through Matrix3's normal bank-inventory wear handler.
9. Click `Examine`. Confirm it still performs Matrix3's normal inventory-item examine behavior.
10. Click bank-inventory-pane `Open`. Confirm it opens the same player-owned backpack storage while validating the physical backpack from the clicked inventory slot.

13. Put 21445 in the main bank item container and right-click it. Confirm the existing configured Bank Option 7 `Open` path still opens the same player-owned backpack storage and is unaffected by `bank_inventory.mode=EXPLICIT`.
14. Right-click ordinary, unconfigured items in the bank inventory pane. Confirm their normal Matrix3 Deposit-5/10/X/All variants remain present and no custom filtering applies to them.
15. Close/reopen the bank repeatedly. Confirm the five configured backpack entries do not duplicate and removed stock variants do not intermittently reappear on 21445.
