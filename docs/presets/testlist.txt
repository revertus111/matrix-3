Gear Presets runtime test list

1. Clean/refresh the Server project in Eclipse with Java 8 and confirm it compiles.
2. Open Gear > Presets and click preset rows 1-10; confirm the preset action dialogue opens instead of repeating "Select a preset row first."
3. Click a row action icon after selecting that preset row and confirm it continues to target the selected preset rather than another row.
4. Save a preset with both worn equipment and backpack items, including stack quantities.
5. Change the carried setup, click Load with no bank open, and confirm loading is rejected without item changes.
6. Open a bank, return to Gear > Presets, load the saved preset, and confirm worn/backpack slot contents and quantities match the saved setup.
7. Verify the displaced setup moves into the bank and required preset items are removed from the bank with no duplicate or missing quantities.
8. Test a nearly/full bank where a one-for-one preset swap should still fit.
9. Test a missing required bank item and insufficient final bank space; both must reject before changing items.
10. Save, logout/restart the server, login, and confirm the preset remains available through the same preset slot.
11. Clear a saved preset and confirm it no longer loads after restart.
12. Test while a controller restricts inventory/equipment changes and confirm the preset load is rejected.
13. Click the Beast of Burden row and confirm it reports that BoB presets are not enabled yet when the cache supplies row 11 in either slot field.
14. Click unrelated/native controls on interface 579 before selecting a preset and confirm they are not spammed with the old selection warning.
15. Restart the server, open Gear > Presets, and click each distinct row/icon/control once. Capture every `[PRESET-MAP]` console line with component, slot, slot2, and packet values.
16. Click the same control repeatedly and confirm an identical click tuple is logged only once per server run.
17. Confirm the mapper itself adds no new game-chat messages. During this diagnostic step, unmapped native preset controls may still have no gameplay action until their component map is wired.
