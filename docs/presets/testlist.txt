Gear Presets runtime test list

1. Clean/refresh the Server project in Eclipse with Java 8 and confirm it compiles.
2. Open Gear > Presets and confirm existing row save/load/delete behavior still works for preset rows 1 and 2.
3. Click at least one matching control on rows 3 and 10 and inspect `Server/data/logs/presets/preset-map.txt`; confirm their component IDs continue the +49 row stride before treating rows 3-10 as VERIFIED.
4. Save a preset with both worn equipment and backpack items, including stack quantities.
5. Click that row's cog and toggle Backpack Off; load the preset and confirm current backpack contents remain untouched while enabled containers change.
6. Toggle Worn equipment Off; load the preset and confirm current equipment remains untouched while enabled containers change.
7. Confirm the cog refuses a state where Backpack, Worn equipment, and Beast of Burden are all Off.
8. Restart the server and confirm per-preset Backpack/Worn/BoB settings persist.
9. Click right radio 1 and right radio 2 on saved rows; confirm the game reports assignment to Quick Preset 1/2 and the assignments persist across restart.
10. Clear a preset assigned to Quick 1 or Quick 2 and confirm the corresponding persisted assignment is cleared.
11. Enable Beast of Burden on a preset saved without an active BoB; with no active BoB, confirm load rejects cleanly without changing bank/inventory/equipment.
12. With a real Beast of Burden active, save a preset containing BoB items, change its contents, then load and confirm the enabled BoB contents restore exactly and the familiar interface refreshes.
13. Verify enabled Backpack/Worn/BoB containers and the bank conserve exact item quantities with no duplicate or missing items.
14. Test a nearly/full bank where a one-for-one preset swap should still fit.
15. Test a missing required bank item and insufficient final bank space; both must reject before changing items.
16. Test while a controller restricts inventory/equipment changes and confirm enabled restricted containers cause the preset load to reject.
17. Save/logout/restart/login and confirm version-2 preset data, container settings, and quick assignments remain available. Existing version-1 preset files should still load with Backpack/Worn enabled and BoB disabled.
18. Click the top Currently Equipped Quick 1 arrow and Quick 2 arrow once each, then click every control on the dedicated bottom Beast of Burden row once; send the resulting new `[PRESET-MAP]` lines so those native controls can be wired without guessing.
19. Confirm `[PRESET-MAP]` output remains de-duplicated per click tuple, appears in the BAT console, and appends to `Server/data/logs/presets/preset-map.txt`.
20. After the preset-only host-order clipping patch, reopen Gear > Presets and confirm the Backpack and Worn panels both render fully. If clipping remains, treat the host-order theory as disproven and do not change global interface scaling.
