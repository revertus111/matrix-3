Gear Presets runtime test list

1. Clean/refresh the Server project in Eclipse with Java 8 and confirm it compiles.
2. Open Gear > Presets and click preset rows 1-10; confirm the preset action dialogue opens.
3. Save a preset with both worn equipment and backpack items, including stack quantities.
4. Change the carried setup, click Load with no bank open, and confirm loading is rejected without item changes.
5. Open a bank, return to Gear > Presets, load the saved preset, and confirm worn/backpack slot contents and quantities match the saved setup.
6. Verify the displaced setup moves into the bank and required preset items are removed from the bank with no duplicate or missing quantities.
7. Test a nearly/full bank where a one-for-one preset swap should still fit.
8. Test a missing required bank item and insufficient final bank space; both must reject before changing items.
9. Save, logout/restart the server, login, and confirm the preset remains available through the same preset slot.
10. Clear a saved preset and confirm it no longer loads after restart.
11. Test while a controller restricts inventory/equipment changes and confirm the preset load is rejected.
12. Click the Beast of Burden row and confirm it reports that BoB presets are not enabled yet.
13. If a native preset icon does not resolve to the selected row, enable DEBUG and capture the PresetManager component/slot/slot2/packet log for that click before extending the mapping.
