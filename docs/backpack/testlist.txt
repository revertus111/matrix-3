Matrix3 Rambler's backpack runtime test list

1. Spawn item 21445 (Rambler's backpack) through the existing owner Item Browser path and equip it. Confirm it occupies the cape/back equipment slot and its worn model renders correctly. If it cannot be worn, record that as cache/equip-definition runtime evidence before changing unrelated equipment code.
2. With 21445 equipped, use the equipment interface's secondary action on the backpack. Confirm interfaces 671 and 665 open as the backpack storage view. Owner fallback test: `::itembrowser backpack open` must open the same view only while the backpack is equipped.
3. Unequip 21445 and run `::itembrowser backpack open`. Confirm the server refuses access with the equip-the-backpack message.
4. Store one normal item, then Store-5, Store-10, and Store-All where quantities permit. Confirm the inventory decreases by exactly the amount added to backpack container key 531.
5. Withdraw one item, then Withdraw-5, Withdraw-10, and Withdraw-All. Confirm the backpack decreases by exactly the amount returned to inventory and partial transfers stop safely when inventory space is limited.
6. Fill the 30 backpack slots with non-stackable items. Confirm another non-stackable store request is refused without deleting or duplicating the source item. Confirm a stackable item can still merge into an existing backpack stack when no free slot remains.
7. Attempt to store item 21445 inside its own backpack. Confirm it is rejected and remains outside the backpack.
8. Use Take All with enough inventory space and confirm all backpack contents move to inventory. Repeat with limited inventory space and confirm only accepted items/amounts move, with no duplication.
9. Close and reopen the backpack repeatedly. Confirm stale clicks do not route to backpack storage after interfaces 671/665 are closed.
10. Summon a real Beast of Burden and use its normal storage before and after testing the backpack. Confirm familiar BoB still uses container key 530 and its existing behavior is unchanged; backpack uses key 531 and does not require or replace a familiar.
11. Store items in the backpack, log out normally, restart/relog, re-equip 21445, and reopen it. Confirm all backpack contents persist exactly.
12. Run the migration test in docs/inventory/testlist.txt for a temporary 36-slot save and confirm former inventory slots 29-36 move once into the backpack without item loss or duplication.
13. Run the normal Matrix3 equipment, inventory, bank, familiar/BoB, and login persistence smoke paths after backpack testing.
