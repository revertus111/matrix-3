BossLabs Arena / Tiles workspace acceptance

1. Eclipse clean/build the Client with Java 8. Confirm BossLabsArenaPanel and the narrow BossLabsPanel draft-access seam compile cleanly.
2. Load a BossLabs draft with at least two phases and multiple attacks. Open Arena / Tiles and confirm the Phase and Attack selectors populate from the same draft used by Attacks/Phases.
3. Switch between phases and attacks in Arena / Tiles. Confirm the canvas and summary always reflect the selected attack's existing tile offsets, impact effect, hazard effect, and hazard timing.
4. Paint tiles with left click. Confirm existing painted tiles toggle off, empty tiles toggle on, DRAFT changes to modified, and offsets outside +/-16 are rejected.
5. Right-click a painted tile. Confirm it erases without adding an empty tile. Middle-drag must pan without changing offsets and mouse wheel must zoom around the cursor.
6. Click Center View after panning/zooming. Confirm target 0,0 returns to the center and zoom resets without changing any authored tiles.
7. Apply Cross, H Line, V Line, Square, and Ring at representative radii including 0, 1, 2, and 16 where valid. Confirm each replaces the selected pattern and remains hand-editable afterward.
8. Attempt a preset that would exceed 128 tiles, such as filled Square radius 6. Confirm the request is rejected before the current pattern is changed.
9. Paint a custom pattern, click Copy Pattern, switch to a different attack or phase, and click Paste Pattern. Confirm the destination receives a deep copy of the geometry and later edits do not mutate the source attack.
10. Clear a pattern on an attack with no hazard/non-default tile requirement. Confirm the attack returns to its normal no-pattern behavior. Clear a pattern while a hazard or non-default impact effect still requires tiles and confirm normal draft validation prevents publish until corrected.
11. Edit a pattern in Arena / Tiles, then switch to Attacks. Confirm the Attacks view reflects the same underlying attack object/tile count and no second draft copy exists.
12. Edit a pattern in Attacks, then switch back to Arena / Tiles or press Refresh. Confirm the arena workspace reloads the current draft geometry rather than stale cached offsets.
13. Save & Apply an arena-edited attack, restart/reinspect, and confirm every explicit tile offset reloads exactly through the existing v8 wire/store path.
14. Apply Live without saving, then trigger the edited attack through Testing. Confirm runtime uses the same target-relative pattern semantics already owned by BossCombatScript.
15. Confirm this slice does not change BossDefinitionStore/BossLabsDefinitionWireCodec v8, BossCombatScript, World/NPC core, Commands.java, packet framing, targeting semantics, or introduce fixed-world arena anchors/zone persistence. The Arena / Tiles tab is a large authoring view over existing persisted attack patterns only.
